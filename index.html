Net gaya to? Koi baat nahi.

Baghbaan ka dimagh kisi company ke server par nahi — tumhare apne computer par chalta hai. Yehi is project ki jaan hai.

Apna model, apni machine

Jawab dene wala AI open-weight model hai jo laptop par hi chalta hai. Signal ho ya na ho — kheth me, chhat par, kahin bhi — Baghbaan jawab dega.

Bilkul muft

Na API key, na monthly bill, na per-sawal paisa. Model ek dafa download karo — phir jitna marzi poocho, hamesha ke liye.

Tumhara data, tumhare paas

Tumhare sawal kahin upload nahi hote. Aur chaho to model ko aur sikhaya bhi ja sakta hai — apni zameen, apne beej, apna AI. Yehi open innovation hai.
